Researcher · Multimodal Learning & Vision-Language

Rabeya Akter

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at North South University, where I work with Md Salman Shamil and Dr. Md Adnan Arefeen on egocentric video understanding, modeling hand-object interactions and everyday task actions with LLM-guided reasoning.

My latest first-author work, EK-Bangla, asks whether multimodal LLMs can describe fine-grained hand-object interactions in Bangla as well as they do in English. Every model we tested does worse in Bangla, and most caption errors are visual rather than linguistic.

I earned my B.Sc. in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Dhaka. After graduating, I worked on amodal counting with Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan (University of Dhaka). As an undergraduate, I worked on continuous Bangla sign language translation with Dr. Shafin Rahman (North South University) and Dr. Sejuti Rahman. In industry, I was an Associate Software Engineer on the machine learning team at Therap (BD) Ltd. and a data science intern at Pathao.

Beyond research, I enjoy mentoring in olympiads, taking part in hackathons, and exploring cultural activities such as writing and performing.

I am actively seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.
