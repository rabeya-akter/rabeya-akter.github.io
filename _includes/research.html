Research

I want models that understand what is happening in the physical world, explain it in the language people actually speak, and act on it. My work follows three threads. To perceive, a model should resolve fine-grained detail, like which hand touches which object, even when evidence is small, occluded or spread over time. To communicate, that grounding has to hold up beyond English, in low-resource languages such as Bangla. To act and reason, models need richer feedback than success or failure: why they failed, and where to look.

01 · Perceive

Egocentric & Multimodal Understanding

I study fine-grained visual understanding, from hand-object interactions in egocentric video to counting objects hidden behind occluders, by fusing complementary modalities so one view fills in what another misses.

EK-Bangla → CountOCC → T3Time →

02 · Communicate

Low-Resource Vision & Language

I build benchmarks and models that test whether visual grounding survives the switch from English to Bangla, in egocentric video captioning and sign language translation.

EK-Bangla → SignFormer-GCN → Bornil →

03 · Act & Reason

Embodied AI & Post-Training

I use vision–language feedback to explain why a robot failed and turn it into rewards, and teach language models which earlier steps to attend to while reasoning.
